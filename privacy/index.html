Privacy Policy

Your data

This policy covers the Rewoven Chrome extension and the Rewoven website.

Effective date: June 2026

The short version: the extension reads product pages on your device, sends only the detected brand name to our API, and keeps nothing about you. The website is a static site. We don't run analytics or trackers in the extension, and Rewoven sets no cookies.

1. The Chrome extension

What happens on your device. When you visit a fashion product page, the extension reads the page's content locally in your browser to detect the brand and, where listed, the fabric composition. That analysis happens entirely on-device. The contents of the pages you visit are never uploaded anywhere.

What gets sent. To fetch a brand's sustainability rating, the extension sends only the detected brand name (as a slug, e.g. patagonia) to our public API at api.rewovenapp.com. That request contains:

  • no personal data,
  • no browsing history,
  • no page URLs or page contents,
  • no identifiers tied to you - the extension requires no account or sign-in.

What we store. Nothing about you, server-side. The API serves the same public brand database to everyone and doesn't keep profiles of who asked for what. Your extension settings (for example, whether the overlay is enabled) stay in your browser's local storage on your device.

No analytics, no trackers. The extension contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

2. The website

rewovenapp.com is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Rewoven sets no cookies. A few practical notes:

  • Your light/dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and hosting is provided by GitHub Pages; like any web servers, those providers may keep standard technical logs on their end under their own policies.
  • If you choose to create a Rewoven account (used by the app and some site features), sign-in is handled by our backend provider, Supabase, and is covered by the consent you give at sign-up. Browsing this site requires no account.

3. Children

Rewoven is built with young people in mind, which is exactly why the extension and website are designed to work without accounts, personal data, or tracking.

4. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. We won't quietly start collecting things this page says we don't.
