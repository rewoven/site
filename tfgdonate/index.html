Guidelines
What We Accept
Please ensure donated items are clean and in wearable condition

We Accept

  • T-shirts, tops, and blouses
  • Jeans, trousers, and shorts
  • Dresses and skirts
  • Jackets and hoodies
  • Children's clothing (all sizes)
  • Shoes in good condition
  • Bags and accessories
  • School uniforms

We Don't Accept

  • Torn or heavily stained items
  • Underwear or swimwear
  • Wet or mouldy clothing
  • Items with strong odours
  • Single socks or broken zippers
  • Pillows, duvets, or curtains

FAQ
Questions?
Everything you need to know
Who is Thrift for Good?▼
Thrift for Good is a UAE-based charity that collects, sorts, and redistributes pre-loved clothing to communities in need.
What is Rewoven's role?▼
Rewoven collects donations in support of Thrift for Good. Drop off your clothes at our locations in Dubai and Sharjah, and we make sure they get to Thrift for Good for redistribution.
Where are the drop-off points?▼
We have two collection points: Dubai and Sharjah.
Where do the clothes go?▼
Thrift for Good sorts all donations and redistributes wearable items to underprivileged communities. Items that can't be worn are responsibly recycled - nothing goes to landfill.
Can I donate from outside the UAE?▼
Currently, drop-off is only available in Dubai and Sharjah. We're working on expanding to more locations.
