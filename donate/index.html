Guidelines
What We Accept
Please ensure donated items are clean and in wearable condition

We Accept

  • T-shirts, tops, and blouses
  • Jeans, trousers, and shorts
  • Dresses and skirts
  • Jackets and hoodies
  • Children's clothing (all sizes)
  • Shoes in good condition
  • Bags and accessories
  • School uniforms

We Don't Accept

  • Torn or heavily stained items
  • Underwear or swimwear
  • Wet or mouldy clothing
  • Items with strong odours
  • Single socks or broken zippers
  • Pillows, duvets, or curtains

FAQ
Questions?
Everything you need to know about the donation drive
Where are the drop-off points?▼
We have two drop-off locations: Dubai and Sharjah. We can also arrange a pickup — just select "Arrange a pickup" in the registration form.
How do I get my handbag or bookmark?▼
After your donation is received and processed, we'll craft your reward and deliver it to you within 2-3 weeks. You'll get a tracking notification via email.
Can I donate from outside the UAE?▼
Currently the drive is focused on the UAE, but you can ship items to us. Select "Ship to Rewoven" in the form and we'll provide a shipping address.
How do I know my clothes reach the children?▼
We provide full transparency. You'll receive photos and updates from our partners in the Philippines showing your donations being distributed to children and families.
What happens to items that can't be worn?▼
Nothing goes to waste. Items not suitable for wearing are repurposed into cleaning cloths, insulation material, or used to create the fabric offcut rewards like handbags and bookmarks.
Is there a deadline?▼
The drive runs on a rolling basis, but we ship in batches. Register early to be part of the next shipment and receive your reward sooner!
