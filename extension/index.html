Chrome Extension

See how sustainable a brand is, right while you shop.

The Rewoven extension overlays a brand's sustainability grade - from our 635-brand database - directly on fashion product pages. When a page lists its fabric composition, it adds material footprint estimates from published research, and it suggests higher-rated alternatives in a similar style and price range.

What It Does

Three things, honestly

No popups, no nagging. Just the information you'd want before checkout.

🏷️

Brand grade on the page

Detects the brand you're browsing and shows its A-F grade and 0-100 score from Rewoven's 635-brand database, with the four dimension scores behind it: environmental impact, labor practices, transparency, and animal welfare.

💧

Material footprint estimates

When a product page lists its fabric composition, the extension calculates estimated water and carbon footprints from published per-fibre research averages. Every estimate is marked with a ~ because that's what it is: an estimate, not a measurement. See the sources.

↻

Higher-rated alternatives

For lower-scoring brands, it suggests better-rated brands in a similar category and price range, so a more sustainable choice is one click away instead of a research project.

Our Promise

It never invents data

Rewoven's whole point is honest information. The extension follows the same rules as everything else we build:

  • ✓If a page doesn't list materials, it says so. You'll see "composition not listed" instead of a guessed footprint. No fabric data means no footprint, ever.
  • ✓Estimates are marked with a ~. Footprint figures are calculated from published research averages and are useful for comparing materials, not for measuring a specific garment. They're always labelled as estimates.
  • ✓Ratings are editorial opinion, with the working shown. Brand grades are Rewoven's sourced assessments, not statements of fact, and every brand includes a "why this score" explanation.

Read exactly how scores and estimates are produced, including every data source, in our public methodology.

Privacy

Your browsing stays yours

The extension reads product pages locally in your browser and sends only the detected brand name to our API to fetch its rating. No personal data, no browsing history, no account, no analytics.
